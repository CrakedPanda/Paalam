Sources, model limits & privacy +

The recognizer compares recorded hand and upper-body motion with ISL examples using temporal nearest-neighbour matching. It is an experimental, isolated-word model. It does not read continuous signing, facial grammar, fingerspelled names or arbitrary sentences. The phrase patterns are an English output aid, not an ISL grammar lesson.

Training/reference sources: ISL500 / ISL-DATA (research and academic use); “name” examples: Indian Sign Language Research and Training Centre, Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India, via a dictionary re-encode. See ISLRTC usage terms and the local model card.

Camera frames stay on this device. Personal landmark examples remain in browser storage until deleted. Sentences and your typed name are kept only in page memory. Local reference clips/model files need no network. Voices marked “online” may send spoken text to their provider.
