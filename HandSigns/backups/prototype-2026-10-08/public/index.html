How it works, privacy & limitations +

MediaPipe estimates 21 hand landmarks locally. Hand-written geometry rules match ten static poses. There is no custom trained gesture model, ISL dataset, or validated accuracy score. Borderline poses, two hands, and partially visible hands are rejected.

A pose must remain stable for 0.7 seconds before it is added and spoken. Held poses speak once; relax for 0.45 seconds or hold a different gesture before repeating. Motion, grammar, facial expression and two-handed signs are not interpreted. Lighting, camera angle, mobility and occlusion can affect results.

Video stays in this browser; it is not recorded or uploaded. The transcript stays in memory and disappears on reload. Vision assets are served locally. Browser voices marked “online” may send spoken text to their provider. Choose a local voice for offline speech.

For authentic ISL recognition, this project needs verified vocabulary and consented, diverse labeled recordings, reviewed with ISL users and evaluated on people excluded from training.
